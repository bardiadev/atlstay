## The Gainesville Lakefront: Marinas, Regattas and a City

This is the working end of Lake Lanier. Aqualand Marina at 6800 Lights Ferry Road is marketed by its operator as the largest inland marina in the country; Port Royale at 8800 Port Royale Drive runs more than 700 slips a few coves over; Gainesville Marina at 2145 Dawsonville Highway is the northernmost public marina on the lake. Together they make this the biggest concentration of slips anywhere on Lanier.

### The Second Season Nobody Prices For

Clarks Bridge Park hosted rowing and canoe/kayak at the 1996 Olympics and is the only Georgia venue from those Games still used for its original sport. It still runs regattas, and a new boathouse opened there in March 2024. Competitive rowing brings crews, coaches and families for several nights at a stretch, booked well ahead, in spring and autumn — precisely when a lake property priced purely for summer boating sits empty. Bass tournaments with marina weigh-ins do something similar.

### Two Rules, and They Are Not the Same Rule

Unincorporated Hall County is the most workable jurisdiction on the lake: Chapter 5.100 permits short-term rentals in all residential districts, defines the stay as two to thirty consecutive nights, and asks for a business licence per property, a 24-hour local contact who responds within an hour, and occupancy of two per bedroom plus three.

Inside the City of Gainesville the position reverses. The city's own code, amended 16 December 2025, defines a short term rental as lodging of not more than 15 days and permits it only in office, business and industrial districts — not in its core residential zones. Two houses a mile apart can therefore sit on opposite sides of that line.

### And the Dock

Corps of Engineers permits run with ownership rather than occupancy, cannot be held by a tenant, cannot have their moorage sublet, and go void on sale or transfer. The Corps capped private dock permits at 10,615 lakewide. Buy the dock with the house.
