## North Beach: Fort Screven, the Lighthouse and the Quiet End

North Beach runs from the top of the island down to around Second and Third Avenue, and it is built on a former US Army coastal defence post. Fort Screven was decommissioned in 1945 and its officers' quarters and gun batteries were converted, leaving an architectural character the rest of Tybee does not have. The Tybee Island Light Station stands here — first built in 1773, 154 feet tall, one of a small number of surviving colonial-era light towers in the country.

### What the Guest Comes For

Quiet, mostly, and history second. This is the least crowded beach on Tybee, and North Beach markets itself against the South End on exactly that basis. The Tybee Island Marine Science Center at 37 Meddin Drive adds family programming — sea turtle rescue and release education among it — that draws bookings outside the peak beach months.

### The Rule That Governs Supply

Since October 2022 the city has issued no new short-term rental certificates in the R-1, R-1-B and R-2 residential districts, which cover most of the island including this end. That freeze has been tested and upheld: Chatham County Superior Court dismissed a property owners' challenge on 6 August 2026, holding that because short-term rentals lack a relatively continuous physical presence, Tybee may regulate them more strictly than ordinary residential rentals.

The practical consequence is blunt. North Beach's short-term rental supply is effectively fixed at whatever was registered before the freeze. If you are buying here to let, the existing certificate is part of what you are buying, and verifying that it exists and is current matters more than almost anything else in the transaction.

### What Changes in 2027

An amendment passed in August 2026 replaces the blanket freeze with a sector system from 1 January 2027. The northern and western residential sectors — this end — are capped at 30 percent of homes, against up to 60 percent in the eastern and southern beach and commercial sectors. The city has said it will run public workshops before it takes effect, and we will be confirming the detail for the properties we manage as it firms up.

### How We Manage It

We verify the certificate first and everything else second. Then we build the listing around what actually distinguishes this end — the lighthouse, the fort, the calm — rather than around proximity to a pier that is a bike ride away. And we run the parking rules properly, because on an island this constrained they are enforced rather than nominal.
