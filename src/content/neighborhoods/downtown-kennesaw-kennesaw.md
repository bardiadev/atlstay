## Downtown Kennesaw: Big Shanty, The General and a Walkable Core

Kennesaw began as Big Shanty, a stop on the Western and Atlantic Railroad, and the 1840s railroad town is still legible in the brick storefronts along Main Street. The Big Shanty Village historic district went onto the National Register in 1980. At its centre sits the depot and the Southern Museum of Civil War and Locomotive History at 2829 Cherokee Street — a Smithsonian Affiliate, and home to The General, the locomotive stolen in the Great Locomotive Chase of 1862.

### Why It Lets

Because people can walk. Main Street is a designated open-container entertainment district, so a group books here in order to move between Horned Owl Brewing, the restaurants and Depot Park on foot. Add the events calendar — a Big Shanty Festival that has run roughly fifty years and brings more than 250 arts and crafts booths, the First Friday Concert Series from May to October, and summer concerts at the Piedmont Bank Amphitheater — and the weekend demand is genuinely event-driven rather than incidental.

### The Rule That Governs It

Kennesaw runs its own short-term rental programme under Chapter 22, Article XIII, with applications opened on 1 October 2025: a business licence, a $250 certificate application, a $90 life-safety inspection, the certificate posted in the unit, an 8 percent hotel/motel excise tax, and a 24-hour local contact, renewed by 31 December each year. The constraints matter more than the fees — two properties per owner, a 250-foot buffer between short-term rentals, and a reported citywide cap of 150 licences. Around 70 pre-existing operators were grandfathered, losing that status on sale, on damage above half the structure, or after twelve months not operating.

In a district this small, the 250-foot buffer is the binding constraint. Whether a certificate is available for your address is a question about your neighbours, not about your house.

### How We Manage It

We check certificate availability for the specific parcel before anything else, because in downtown that answer is genuinely uncertain. Where a certificate is available we run the property for the events calendar and the walkability.
