## Unicoi and Anna Ruby Falls: The Trees Half of Helen

Unicoi State Park runs to 1,050 acres around a 53-acre lake about two miles from downtown Helen on GA-356, with roughly twelve miles of trail, a Georgia DNR lodge, cottages and a campground. At its northern end sits the Anna Ruby Falls scenic area, run by the US Forest Service — twin waterfalls formed where Curtis Creek and York Creek meet, reached by a short paved trail from the visitor centre.

### A Different Guest From Downtown

The visitor who comes here has usually either done Helen already or has no interest in it. They want the falls, the lake and the trail network, and a private cabin rather than a campsite or a lodge room. The five-mile Smith Creek Trail connects the lake area to the falls, which means a guest can walk the thing they came for from a property nearby.

That distinction matters commercially. A cabin out here should be marketed on trail access and quiet, and should be honest about the drive into town. Sold as walkable to Main Street when it is not, it earns the review that costs the next six bookings.

### The Rulebook Is the County's

This is the part owners most often get wrong, so it is worth stating precisely. We checked the park's coordinates against the Census Bureau's own boundary for the City of Helen, having first validated the method on Helen City Hall, which correctly returned Helen city. The park falls outside every incorporated place, as does the Anna Ruby Falls visitor centre. Both are unincorporated White County.

So the City of Helen's lodging registration and its 8 percent city lodging tax do not reach here. What does is White County's Short-Term Rental Host Licence, in force since November 2022, with a countywide ceiling of 650 active licences, a responsible party reachable around the clock who must answer a complaint within two hours, insurance, and annual renewal on 31 December. Since 31 January 2024 a new short-term rental inside a platted subdivision needs that subdivision's covenants to permit it.

### How We Manage It

We settle the covenant and the county eligibility position for the specific parcel before anything else, because on this side of the line the answer is genuinely not guaranteed. Then we price the autumn properly — Oktoberfest runs roughly seven weeks from mid-September into November and is the single largest event in this market's year — and we run the listing on the falls and the lake rather than on a walk to Main Street that does not exist.
