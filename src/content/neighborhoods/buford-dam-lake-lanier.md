## Buford Dam: The Reason the Lake Exists

Buford Dam impounds the Chattahoochee to create Lake Lanier, and the south end of the lake takes its character from that fact rather than from any town. The Corps of Engineers runs its Lake Lanier Project Management Office at 1050 Buford Dam Road and operates Buford Dam Park as a day-use site. Bald Ridge Marina at 1850 Bald Ridge Marina Road in Cumming is the southernmost marina on the water.

### A Different Guest Entirely

Below the dam, the Corps releases cold water drawn from the bottom of the reservoir, and that release creates a tailwater trout fishery that holds rainbow and brown trout right through a Georgia summer. It is a nationally known fishery running through the edge of metro Atlanta, reached from points like the Lower Pool West ramp and Bowmans Island. The visitor it brings is a fly angler, not a pontoon family, and they come in months when the lake itself is quiet.

This is also the closest shoreline to Atlanta, which shapes booking behaviour: more short-notice weekends, fewer planned weeks.

### One Warning That Belongs in the Listing

The Corps generates on an unpredictable daily schedule and the river below the dam can rise three to four feet in under an hour. Any guest who intends to wade needs to be told that before they arrive, not after. We put it in the house manual and the pre-arrival message.

### The Rules

The shoreline here is Forsyth County, whose ordinance is reported to confine short-term rentals to agricultural and agricultural-residential zoning with a conditional use permit — considerably stricter than Hall County across the lake. We were not able to read that ordinance directly and say so rather than pretending otherwise; it is the first thing we settle on a specific parcel.

On docks, the Corps is explicit: permits run with ownership and not occupancy, a tenant cannot hold one, moorage cannot be sublet, and a permit becomes void on sale or transfer. With the lakewide cap set at 10,615 permits, the dock comes with the house or it does not come at all.

### How We Manage It

We price two seasons rather than one — the summer lake calendar and the winter and spring trout calendar — and we settle the zoning and dock position before a property is listed. Where a house cannot be let nightly under Forsyth's rule, a furnished monthly product still works well this close to Atlanta.
