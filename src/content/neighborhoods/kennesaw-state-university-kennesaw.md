## The KSU Corridor: A College Town Outside the City Limits

The Chastain Road corridor is universally called Kennesaw and is not, legally, in it. We checked Kennesaw State University's main campus at 1000 Chastain Road against the Census Bureau's own place boundaries: it returns no incorporated-place match, sitting instead in its own statistical place within unincorporated Cobb County. The City of Kennesaw's annual financial report confirms it from the other direction — the university appears nowhere on the city's list of principal employers, which would be impossible for an institution of this size inside the city.

That is not pedantry. It decides which short-term rental ordinance governs your property.

### The Scale of the Demand

Kennesaw State enrolled 51,375 students in autumn 2025, a 7.4 percent rise in a single year. A university that size generates its own visitor economy: move-in weekends, family weekend, graduation, prospective-student tours, visiting collegiate teams, and academic conferences. Fifth Third Bank Stadium at 3200 George Busbee Parkway, open since May 2010, hosts KSU football, soccer and lacrosse alongside Atlanta United 2 matches. The Convocation Center adds concerts, commencements and camps.

### Supply Is Coming, But Not Your Supply

Two purpose-built projects are landing: The Standard at Kennesaw at 112 Townpark Drive, 656 beds across 157 units for the 2028-29 academic year, and KSU's own Summit II, roughly 460 beds in about 97,000 square feet opening autumn 2026. Both are student housing. They compete for the student, not for the parent flying in for graduation or the visiting researcher on a four-month appointment — which is the guest a furnished house is actually selling to.

### How We Manage It

We confirm the parcel's jurisdiction first, because the Cobb County ordinance and the City of Kennesaw ordinance are different instruments with different fees, buffers and caps, and applying to the wrong one wastes a season. Then we build the calendar around the university's, price the event dates properly rather than flat, and keep a furnished monthly product underneath it so the property is not idle between graduations.
