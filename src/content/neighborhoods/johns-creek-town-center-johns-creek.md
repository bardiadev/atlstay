## Johns Creek Town Center: The City's First Walkable District

Johns Creek was incorporated in 2006 and, until recently, had no downtown. The Town Center project is the deliberate correction: a 192-acre vision and plan adopted by the city in 2021 to convert an ageing 1980s office park at Medlock Bridge Road, McGinnis Ferry Road and Johns Creek Parkway into something people can walk around.

### What Is Actually Being Built

Two things, side by side. Medley is a $560 million, 43-acre mixed-use development by Toro Development Company on the former State Farm campus, carrying 200,000 square feet of retail and dining, office space and 900 residential units. Next to it, the city built The Boardwalk at Town Center — a 20-acre park costing $39 million with a 4,000-capacity amphitheatre — and a $7 million pedestrian tunnel beneath Medlock Bridge Road so the two connect without crossing traffic.

For a city whose housing market has always been sold on schools and lot size, that is a genuine change in what a property here can be positioned against.

### What It Means for Letting

Two things, pulling in different directions. The amphitheatre and the restaurant district create demand on dates that used to be dead, and they give a furnished let a story beyond the commute. At the same time, 900 new apartments arriving in one district is real competing supply, and it will be newer than nearly everything around it.

The constraint on capturing the first is the city's own zoning. Johns Creek defines a Dwelling as residential use "for periods of more than 30 consecutive days," which means a nightly listing beside the amphitheatre is not a permitted use however tempting the event calendar looks. The workable products are a furnished thirty-day-plus let and a straightforward long-term lease.

### How We Manage It

We position Town Center properties on walkability, which is the one thing no other part of Johns Creek can currently offer, and we sell that to the guest who values it most: the relocating professional at Alcon, Emory Johns Creek Hospital or Technology Park who would rather walk to dinner than drive.
