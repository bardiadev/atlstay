## Sautee Nacoochee: The Real Thing, Three Miles South

The Nacoochee and Sautee valleys sit a few miles south of Helen on GA-17, and they are the opposite of what Main Street sells. Where downtown is a Bavarian theme applied in the 1960s, this is authentic Appalachian Georgia: a Mississippian-period mound roughly seventeen feet high at the valley's western edge, an 1876 water-powered gristmill still selling stone-ground grits, a nineteenth-century farm run by the state as a historic site, and two National Register historic districts that exist because a community association fought through the 1970s and 80s to preserve real farmhouses and churches.

### What Brings Guests

Hardman Farm State Historic Site on GA-17 runs tours seven days a week from March to December. The Sautee Nacoochee Center at 283 Highway 255 North — a nonprofit campus of 8.5 acres built around the 1928 Nacoochee School — holds the Folk Pottery Museum of Northeast Georgia, a 98-seat theatre, an African American Heritage Site and a history museum. Habersham Winery and Creekstone on Hardman Farm Road puts wine tourism inside the valley. The Old Sautee Store at 2315 GA-17 has been trading as a general store for well over a century.

That mix pulls a specific guest: history and arts visitors, wedding parties, and people on their second or third trip to the area who have already done Helen.

### The Rulebook Is the County's

We geocoded Hardman Farm against the Census Bureau's own boundaries — after validating the method on Helen City Hall, which correctly returned Helen city — and it falls outside every incorporated place, in White County. So Helen's lodging registration and its 8 percent city lodging tax do not apply here.

What applies is White County's Short-Term Rental Host Licence: in force since November 2022, capped at 650 active licences countywide, requiring insurance and a responsible party who answers a complaint within two hours, non-transferable, renewing each 31 December. Since 31 January 2024, a new short-term rental inside a platted subdivision needs that subdivision's covenants to allow it.

### How We Manage It

Covenant and eligibility first, because the county genuinely can say no. Then we market the valley on what it actually is rather than as a cheaper Helen — the mound, the mill, the farm, the pottery museum and the winery are the reason someone chooses here, and a listing that leads with "minutes from Helen" sells the wrong thing to the wrong guest. Autumn still governs the calendar: Oktoberfest runs roughly seven weeks from mid-September, and it fills the valley as surely as it fills Main Street.
