## Town Center: The Corridor That Isn't Actually Kennesaw

Everything along Barrett Parkway carries a Kennesaw mailing address and almost none of it is in the city. We checked Town Center at Cobb Mall, at 400 Ernest W Barrett Parkway, against the Census Bureau's own place boundaries: unincorporated Cobb County. It sits inside the Town Center Area Community Improvement District, described by Cobb County itself as a self-taxing district in unincorporated Cobb, established in 1997 and covering 6.25 square miles.

For a property owner that single fact decides the rulebook: Cobb County's short-term rental ordinance, not the City of Kennesaw's.

### What the District Actually Is

A big-box, hotel and office corridor at the I-75 and I-575 interchange. The mall anchors it with Macy's, Belk and JCPenney. Around it sits an unusually dense cluster of hotel brands, most of them extended-stay in character — which is the most useful signal an owner can have, because it means somebody has already proved there is multi-week demand here.

### The Repositioning

The mall was bought out of foreclosure by an affiliate of Ardent Cos. for around $51.8 million, reported by the Atlanta Journal-Constitution on 13 July 2026; the Macy's, JCPenney and former Sears parcels are separately owned, and the old Sears sold on its own for $9 million in March 2025. The Community Improvement District's Livable Centers Initiative plan is feeding a new 30-year master plan, and South Barrett Reliever Phase III — the district's largest infrastructure project to date — is projected to cut peak congestion on Barrett Parkway by as much as 22 percent.

### How We Manage It

We confirm the parcel sits in unincorporated Cobb, apply to the right government, and then price the property against the extended-stay hotel rate rather than a nightly one — because on any stay longer than a couple of weeks that is the real competitor, and it is a comparison a furnished house wins on kitchen, laundry and separate bedrooms.
