## Lake Blue Ridge: 3,290 Acres and a Different Letting Logic

Lake Blue Ridge is a TVA reservoir of about 3,290 acres with roughly 65 miles of shoreline, impounded by the Blue Ridge Dam — 175 feet high, 1,553 feet across, built between 1925 and 1931 and generating 16 megawatts. Only about a quarter of the shoreline is developed, which is the single most important fact for an owner here: a lake-view or dock-access property is genuinely scarce.

### What the Guest Is Buying

Water, not walkability. Downtown Blue Ridge sells a Main Street and a scenic railway; the lake sells a boat. Lake Blue Ridge Marina at 335 Marina Drive off Old Highway 76 hires pontoons, kayaks and paddleboards, stores boats and runs a bar and grill. Morganton Point Recreation Area at 475 Lake Drive gives the US Forest Service 43 campsites, sixteen of them lakefront, whose overflow families reliably book cabins nearby. Swimming, waterskiing and jet skis are all permitted, so the guest profile is wider than anglers alone.

### The Rulebook Is the County's, Not the City's

Most of this shoreline is unincorporated Fannin County, which means Ordinance 2025-02, the county's Short-Term Vacation Rental Ordinance, adopted and effective 26 August 2025. It requires an Accommodation Excise Tax Certificate at $225 to apply and $225 annually to renew, plus a 6 percent county accommodation excise tax, and it imposes no cap on certificates and no zoning-district restriction — a genuine contrast with the ordinance inside the City of Blue Ridge, which confines short-term rentals to particular districts.

The safety schedule is specific and enforced: detectors in every bedroom and on every floor, an extinguisher on every level, reflective 911 signage at the driveway, working internet or cellular service or a dedicated emergency phone, one party per dwelling, and a contact reachable around the clock who responds within two hours. Operating without a certificate runs to $1,000 a day.

### How We Manage It

We confirm the parcel's jurisdiction first — Morganton Point, for instance, sits inside the separately incorporated City of Morganton, not unincorporated Fannin. Then we run the county's safety schedule as a standing checklist rather than an annual scramble, treat septic inspection as scheduled work because a confirmed failure suspends the certificate outright, and price dock access and lake views as the distinct products they are.
