## Brookhaven Village: The Walkable Part of Brookhaven

Brookhaven Village is what has grown up along Dresden Drive just east of the Brookhaven/Oglethorpe MARTA station — a low-rise, mixed-use main street replacing what used to be strip retail. Village Place Brookhaven at 1418 Dresden Drive and Parkside on Dresden at 1350 Dresden Drive are the named examples: shops and restaurants at ground level, apartments and townhomes above.

### Why It Lets Well

Transit and food, in that order. The MARTA station has been on the Gold Line since December 1984 and gives this district something almost nothing else in Brookhaven has: a tenant can genuinely live here without a car. Dresden Drive supplies the evening reason to stay. The city's own comprehensive plan frames the Village as the commercial node serving the Ashford Park and Drew Valley neighbourhoods around it, which is precisely why the walk-to-everything proposition holds.

### Who Rents Here

The employment behind it is substantial for a city this size. Children's Healthcare of Atlanta employs 3,090 people in Brookhaven, AT&T Services 1,979 and AT&T Mobility Services 1,064 — over 6,100 jobs across three employers, all placing staff on assignments measured in months. Oglethorpe University, roughly a mile north, adds visiting academics and families on a separate calendar.

### What the Permit Rules Allow

Brookhaven permits short-term rentals, but Section 27-629(3) confines them to owner-occupiers who have owned the property for more than a year and hold a DeKalb County homestead exemption, bars business entities entirely, allows one permit per owner, caps letting at 180 days a year, and limits permits to five percent of residential parcels per council district. For an investor that is a closed door. For someone letting their own home part of the year it is workable and cheap at $25 annually.

### How We Manage It

For most owners here the answer is a furnished thirty-day-plus let or a long-term lease, positioned on the station and the street rather than on square footage.
