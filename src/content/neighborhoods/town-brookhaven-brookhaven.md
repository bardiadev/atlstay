## Town Brookhaven: Retail, a University and 1,500 Homes

Town Brookhaven opened in 2009 as a 600,000 square foot, 54-acre mixed-use district at Peachtree Road and Town Boulevard. Costco arrived in October 2010. Publix, Marshalls, an LA Fitness and a Cobb Theatres CineBistro fill out the anchors, and more than 1,500 residences are built into the complex itself. Oglethorpe University sits immediately next door.

### Why It Produces Letting Demand

Because of who works and studies within a few minutes of it. The City of Brookhaven's financial report lists Children's Healthcare of Atlanta first among the city's employers at 3,090 people, AT&T Services second at 1,979 and AT&T Mobility Services third at 1,064 — better than 6,100 jobs across three employers. Autotrader adds 332. Oglethorpe University brings a separate flow of visiting families, prospective students and academics on the academic calendar rather than the corporate one.

### The Permit Rule That Decides Your Strategy

Brookhaven does allow short-term rentals, which makes it unusual among its neighbours — but the terms decide almost everything. Section 27-629(3), adopted 23 April 2019, limits applicants to owner-occupiers who have owned the property for more than a year and hold a current DeKalb County homestead exemption. A permit cannot be issued to a business entity of any kind, including an LLC or a family trust. One permit per owner, 180 days a year maximum, $25 annually, and a cap at five percent of the residential parcels in each council district.

In practice: if you live in the property, a permit is worth having. If you do not, the route is a long-term lease or a furnished let of thirty days or more — which is what the medical, corporate and university demand around Town Brookhaven wants anyway.
