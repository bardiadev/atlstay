## Georgetown: Dunwoody's Largest Residential Quarter

Georgetown occupies the southern edge of Dunwoody, bounded by Chamblee Dunwoody Road to the west, Cotillion Drive to the south, Peachford Road to the east and Brook Run Park to the north. The city's own planning work treats a 368-acre Georgetown and North Shallowford area as a single study zone mixing commercial, residential and institutional uses.

### What Makes It Different

Where Perimeter Center is towers and Dunwoody Village is a retail crossroads, Georgetown is simply where a large number of people live. Lot sizes and house eras vary across it, the resident community is active, and the recreation anchors — Brook Run Park along the northern edge and the Georgetown Recreation Center within it — do the work that a commercial district does elsewhere.

### Who Rents Here

Families and settled households, overwhelmingly. The letting proposition is space, park access and an easy run to I-285, and the tenants who respond to that are the ones who renew. A smaller furnished market runs alongside it, fed by Peachford Hospital on Peachford Road, which employs 338 people and generates the usual mix of contract clinical staff and visiting families, and by Perimeter Center's much larger payroll a short drive north — InterContinental Hotels Group alone at 2,511.

### The Rule That Applies

As everywhere in the city, short-term rentals are not permitted. Dunwoody stated it plainly in the council packet for its January 2022 Chapter 27 amendment, which also created a Party House use and banned it in residential districts city-wide, allowing it only by special administrative permit in certain non-residential districts for a maximum of 48 hours. There is no registration to complete. A furnished thirty-day-plus let and a conventional lease are the lawful routes, and both suit this area well.

### How We Manage It

Most Georgetown properties are twelve-month lettings and should be, with a tenant screened against a written income standard and a photographed move-in condition report on file from day one. Where a house sits close enough to Peachford or to the Perimeter employers to justify it, we will price a furnished option alongside and let the numbers decide.
