## Mid Beach: Where the Week-Long Bookings Are

Mid Beach runs roughly from 1st Street to 14th Street along Butler Avenue, the island's main residential spine. It has no lighthouse and no pier. That absence is the point: nothing routes day-trip traffic through it, and Visit Tybee's own material describes the stretch as the one most used by Tybee locals and by families renting vacation homes.

### The Commercial Logic

This is the island's long-stay inventory. The guest here is booking a week, sometimes more, and choosing Mid Beach precisely because they want a quieter beach within walking or cycling distance of the South End's restaurants. For an owner that is the most valuable booking shape on the island — one changeover instead of three, a lower cleaning burden, and a guest who is living in the property rather than passing through it.

The housing supports it. Mid Beach is predominantly single-family cottages along and off Butler Avenue, in more standard vacation-home layouts than North Beach's smaller historic stock or the South End's denser condos and inns.

### The Supply Constraint

The same freeze applies here as at the north end. Since October 2022 Tybee has issued no new short-term rental certificates in its R-1, R-1-B and R-2 residential districts, and Chatham County Superior Court upheld the city's authority to do so on 6 August 2026, dismissing a property owners' challenge on the basis that short-term rentals lack a relatively continuous physical presence.

So the certificate attached to a Mid Beach house is not a formality you can arrange after closing. It is part of the asset, and confirming it is current is the first piece of diligence rather than the last.

### And What Changes

From 1 January 2027 a sector system replaces the blanket freeze: the western and northern residential sectors capped at 30 percent of homes, the eastern and southern beach and commercial sectors at up to 60 percent. The amendment passed in August 2026 and the city has said it will hold workshops before it starts.

### How We Manage It

We set the property up for the seven-night family booking, because that is what this stretch actually sells, and we price the calendar accordingly rather than chasing two-night weekends that suit the South End better. Parking rules get enforced attention — vehicles must sit in the driveway or designated area with a pass displayed — because on Tybee that is a real source of complaints and complaints are what put a certificate at risk.
