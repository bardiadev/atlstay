## Historic Brookhaven: Estate Housing and a 1911 Golf Course

Historic Brookhaven was platted in 1910 around what became the Capital City Club's golf course — laid out in 1911, expanded to eighteen holes in 1915, with a clubhouse dating from 1928. It is Georgia's earliest planned golf-club community, and it still reads that way: curvilinear streets, large wooded lots, and estate homes built largely before the Second World War, a number of them by the Atlanta architects Hal Hentz and Neel Reid. Country Club Estates, subdivided in 1929, runs along Winall Down Road, Vermont, Stovall, Calvert Lane and Narmore.

### A Different Letting Market

Nothing about this area behaves like the rest of Brookhaven. There is no retail, no station and no density. What there is instead is scarce, large, architecturally distinctive housing, and the tenant who wants it is a senior person arriving in Atlanta for a defined period.

The employment supports that. Children's Healthcare of Atlanta is the city's largest employer at 3,090 people and AT&T's Services and Mobility Services entities employ 3,043 between them. Organisations of that size relocate senior staff, and senior staff relocating with a family want a house on a large lot near a good club, not a corporate apartment.

### The Permit Position

Brookhaven allows short-term rentals under Section 27-629(3), adopted 23 April 2019, but the terms confine them to owner-occupiers who have held the property more than a year and carry a DeKalb County homestead exemption. Business entities — LLCs, partnerships, family trusts — cannot hold a permit at all. One per owner, 180 days a year, capped at five percent of residential parcels per council district. For most owners of investment property here that closes the question, and the sensible products are a long-term lease or a furnished executive let of thirty days or more.

### How We Manage It

We treat Historic Brookhaven as a small, high-value market rather than a volume one. That means a twelve-month lease as the base case, a furnished executive placement as the upside when the right tenant appears, careful screening in both cases, and a documented condition record — which matters more, not less, when the property is irreplaceable.
