## The Battery and Cumberland: Cobb's Event Engine

The Battery Atlanta opened in 2017 alongside Truist Park and functions less like a neighbourhood than a self-contained entertainment campus: three million square feet of retail, dining, offices, hotels and 531 residences wrapped around a 41,084-seat ballpark. In July 2025 it hosted the MLB All-Star Game.

### The Boundary Nobody Expects

Truist Park's postal address says Atlanta. Its actual jurisdiction, checked against the Census Bureau's own place boundaries, is unincorporated Cobb County — not Atlanta, and not Smyrna. Smyrna's residential streets begin a few minutes away, and the line between them is exactly where your regulatory position changes: Smyrna's own ordinance on one side, Cobb County's on the other, with different fees, caps and day limits.

### What Drives the Demand

Baseball, first: 81 home games a season plus any postseason, against a 41,084-seat capacity. Then the Coca-Cola Roxy, a 3,600-capacity concert venue on the site, filling nights the ballpark does not. Then the offices — Comcast's regional headquarters sits in One Ballpark Center — which produce weekday corporate demand on a completely different rhythm. Braves Development Co. added to that in 2025 by acquiring Pennant Park, six buildings across 34 acres at the I-75 and I-285 interchange.

### How We Manage It

Pricing is the whole game here. A property near this district that charges a flat nightly rate across a calendar containing 81 home games, a concert schedule and the occasional All-Star Game is leaving money on the table every single week.
