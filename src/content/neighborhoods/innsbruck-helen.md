## Innsbruck: A Resort Community, and Legally Part of Helen

Innsbruck sits on the hillside above downtown: villas and townhomes wrapped around an eighteen-hole course that opened in 1987, designed by Bill Watts at 6,748 yards, governed since the development era by the Innsbruck Property Owners Association. Its internal streets — Bahn Innsbruck, Schwarzwald Strasse — mirror the Strasse grid downtown.

### The Boundary Finding That Changes the Answer

Innsbruck is marketed as a secluded resort and feels like one, which is why owners routinely assume it follows the same county rules as the cabins around Helen. It does not. We geocoded two separate addresses inside the development — the golf clubhouse and a villa deeper in — against the Census Bureau's own boundaries, having validated the method on Helen City Hall. **Both returned Helen city.**

So while a cabin at Unicoi or in Sautee Nacoochee falls under White County's host licence and its 650-licence countywide cap, Innsbruck answers to the City of Helen instead: an occupational tax certificate and a separate lodging registration filed per property at City Hall, 8 percent city lodging tax, a monthly hotel/motel tax report due by the 20th even when collections are zero, and a local contact who responds to a complaint within two hours.

Worth knowing: Helen's occupational tax is the city's standard business tax, tiered by employee count from $350 with a $15 administration fee. Several short-term rental sites present that figure as a rental-specific charge. It is not. And we found no numeric licence cap in the city's own materials — a real difference from the county's ceiling.

### A Different Product

This is villa and townhome stock with a pool, tennis courts and a golf course attached, not a cabin in the trees. It sells to golf parties and to groups who have outgrown a one-bedroom chalet, and some units sit inside a timeshare exchange network that delivers guests who never touch Airbnb or Vrbo at all.

The association's recorded by-laws contemplate rental use — they provide for commercial space including a rental or leasehold motel facility — which is a good starting position. It is not a substitute for reading the current documents, and we do that for the specific unit before quoting.

### How We Manage It

City registration and the monthly tax report get run as a standing calendar item, because the filing is due whether or not the unit earned anything that month and a missed one is the easy way to a penalty. We price golf and the festival calendar as two separate seasons, and we treat the exchange network as a real channel rather than an afterthought where a unit is enrolled in one.
