## Perimeter Center: An Edge City Inside a Suburb

Perimeter Center does not look like the rest of Dunwoody and does not behave like it either. Office towers and high-rise residential cluster around Perimeter Mall at the intersection of I-285 and GA-400, served by their own MARTA rail station. Everywhere else in Dunwoody is single-family; this is the exception, and it is where the city's letting demand concentrates.

### The Employers Behind the Demand

The City of Dunwoody's annual financial report for the year ended 31 December 2024 lists InterContinental Hotels Group first among the city's employers at 2,511 people, based at its Americas headquarters on Three Ravinia Drive. Hapag-Lloyd America follows at 763, the DeKalb County School District at 629, T-Mobile at 509 and Peachford Hospital at 338. Perimeter Mall LLC is separately the city's largest property taxpayer, at just under $160 million of assessed value — about 4.25 percent of the entire city.

That is a hospitality multinational, a global shipping line, a telecoms operator and a hospital inside one district. Each of them relocates people, and each of them does it on a timescale measured in months.

### What You Can and Cannot Do Here

Dunwoody does not permit short-term rentals. The city said so directly in the council packet for its January 2022 Chapter 27 amendment — "Short-term rentals of residential properties are not allowed in the City of Dunwoody" — and that same amendment created a Party House use and banned it in residential districts city-wide. There is no registration process to complete, because there is nothing to register.

This matters less than it sounds. Perimeter Center's demand was never nightly. It is corporate, clinical and relocation demand, and it is served by exactly the products the code does permit: a furnished let of thirty days or more, or a straightforward lease.
