## Dunwoody Village: The Low-Rise Alternative

Dunwoody Village has looked broadly the same since 1970. The city's original commercial core sits at Chamblee Dunwoody Road and Mount Vernon Road, built to a deliberate colonial Williamsburg standard — brick, white columns, cupolas — and it has stayed low-rise while Perimeter Center a few minutes north grew towers.

### What Anchors It

The Dunwoody Village Shopping Center at 5501 Chamblee Dunwoody Road, anchored by The Fresh Market and including the Dunwoody post office, gives the district something Perimeter does not have: a daily rhythm. People come here for groceries and dinner rather than for a flight or a quarterly review. Around it sit traditional single-family streets in the Vanderlyn Elementary School and Dunwoody High School catchments.

### Who Rents Here

Two groups. The larger is families renting for the schools, who tend to stay for years — the most valuable tenant a landlord can have and the reason most Village properties are best let unfurnished on a twelve-month term. The smaller but well-paid group is corporate: Perimeter Center's employers, led by InterContinental Hotels Group at 2,511 people, Hapag-Lloyd America at 763 and T-Mobile at 509, place staff who can afford a tower and would rather have a house.

### The Rule That Governs Both

Dunwoody does not allow short-term rentals — the city said so in terms in its January 2022 Chapter 27 amendment, which also created and banned a Party House use in residential districts city-wide. There is no permit to obtain because there is no process. The lawful products here are a long-term lease and a furnished let of thirty days or more, and between the school catchment and the Perimeter payroll, both have real demand behind them.

### How We Manage It

We look at the address before recommending a strategy, because in the Village the two options genuinely diverge. A house deep in the Vanderlyn catchment is a twelve-month letting; one within walking distance of the shopping centre and a short drive of Ravinia can often earn more furnished. We screen against a written standard either way, and we document condition at move-in, because that record is what settles a deposit dispute later.
