## South Beach: The Pier, the Strip and the Growth Zone

South Beach is four blocks doing most of Tybee's commercial work. The pier and pavilion sit at the foot of Tybrisa Street, the restaurant and bar strip runs off it, and most of the island's hotels and public parking are packed into the same few blocks between 14th and 18th Street.

### Why It Trades Differently

Two reasons, and the second is the one that matters to an investor.

The first is demand shape. This is the part of the island a guest can stay in without a car, and its calendar is event-driven rather than purely seasonal: the pier's fishing and fireworks programme, Tybee Island Pirate Fest each October, and New Year's Eve fireworks that create a real winter peak on a beach island that would otherwise go quiet.

The second is supply. Tybee's short-term rental freeze, in place since October 2022 and upheld by Chatham County Superior Court on 6 August 2026, applies to the R-1, R-1-B and R-2 residential districts. It does not close off the business-zoned core. That makes the South End the one part of the island where new short-term rental supply can still legally be added — and the sector system arriving on 1 January 2027 keeps it that way, permitting up to 60 percent of homes in the eastern and southern sectors against 30 percent in the residential north and west.

### The Caveat Worth Stating

Certificates attach to addresses, not to zones. Being in the right district is necessary and not sufficient, and we confirm the position on the specific parcel before anyone commits to a purchase on the strength of the zoning alone.

### How We Manage It

We price the calendar rather than the season, because Pirate Fest, the pier programme and New Year's Eve are worth considerably more than a flat rate captures. We keep the parking within the property's permitted capacity with passes displayed, since this is the district where that is genuinely policed. And we track the 2027 sector rules as the city runs its workshops, because the properties that adjust early will be the ones that are not scrambling in January.
