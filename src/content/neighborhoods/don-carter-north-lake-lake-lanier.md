## North Lanier: The Quiet End

Don Carter State Park opened to the public on 15 July 2013 at 5000 North Browning Bridge Road, and it remains the only Georgia state park with direct access to Lake Lanier. It sits at the point where Hall County's long shoreline gives way to Dawson County's much smaller share, and it reads as the edge of the lake rather than its centre.

### What Makes It Different

Less of everything the south end has too much of. Fewer boats, fewer docks, more forest, and a Georgia DNR campground where the rest of the lake has private marinas. The park runs 44 developed campsites with electric hook-ups and 16 primitive sites, two boat ramps with courtesy docks, a sand swim beach with a bathhouse, and seasonal kayak, paddleboard and aquacycle hire.

That last detail matters commercially: a guest without a boat can still have a full day here, which widens the market well beyond boat owners.

### Two Counties, Two Rulebooks

Most of this shoreline is unincorporated Hall County, and Hall is the most accommodating jurisdiction on the lake — short-term rentals permitted in all residential districts under Chapter 5.100, with a business licence per property, a 24-hour local contact responding within the hour, and occupancy of two per bedroom plus three.

Across the line, Dawson County works differently. Sections 30-500 to 30-528 require a permit, cap overnight occupancy at two per bedroom plus two with daytime occupancy at one and a half times that, allow only one site-built residence per parcel to be let, and apply an 8 percent excise tax. An amendment effective 18 September 2025 barred manufactured homes, mobile homes, shipping containers and tiny-house products from short-term rental use, grandfathering those already permitted.

### The Dock Question

The same Corps rules apply here as everywhere on Lanier: a permit runs with ownership rather than occupancy, a tenant cannot hold one, moorage cannot be sublet, and the permit is void on sale or transfer. Dock permits are capped at 10,615 lakewide. The one advantage on this end is less competition for shoreline generally — but the cap is a lake-wide cap, so it binds here too.

### How We Manage It

We lead on the quiet, because that is what the returning guest is actually buying and it holds a rate. We confirm which county the parcel sits in before quoting, since Hall and Dawson genuinely differ. And we build the listing so that a family without a boat can still picture a full weekend, which is the difference between a summer-only property and a year-round one.
